## What sharing exposes

Your linked provider sees your first name, patient identifier and sharing status.
You can separately enable mood entries (including intensity, triggers and coping
responses), journal summaries (title/type/word count/date only), quiz results, and
provider notes. Journal bodies, transcripts and recordings are never shared by
these provider endpoints. Providers may assign resources and generate PDFs from
currently authorized categories. The provider roster remains visible while linked
even with all categories off. Your name and identifier can appear on reports.

We also process provider identity/practice/license/timezone, credentials, links,
assignments, reports, and access history (action, resource identifiers, timestamp,
IP and User-Agent). Dashboard auditing records the bridge's IP rather than a
verified browser IP. Account passwords are hashed; the encrypted browser cookie
contains a backend credential and is unavailable to browser JavaScript.

## Purpose, recipients and legal grounds

The purpose is requested sharing, care-support workflows, service security and

## Your controls and retention

In My Provider, turn off any category or revoke the link. New authorized requests
respect that change; information already viewed, copied or downloaded cannot be
recalled. Withdrawal does not automatically delete provider records or erase
earlier lawful processing. Reconnecting starts with all sharing categories off.
You can inspect provider access history in the app.
