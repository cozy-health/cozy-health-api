# Conditional processing agreement templates

Unapproved v0.1, 2026-10-09. Execute only after counsel determines the correct
roles. Parties, contacts, dates, annexes and signatures are required. A BAA and
GDPR DPA serve different regimes; one does not replace the other.

## BAA review template (only where applicable)

Covered entity: [practice]. Business associate: [Cozy Health entity]. Underlying
services: [agreement/date]. PHI categories and permitted purposes: [annex].

- Use/disclose PHI only for agreed services or as legally required; establish
  minimum necessary rules and limits on administration/aggregation purposes.
- Apply Privacy/Security Rule obligations appropriate to the role; identify the
  actual safeguards and required vendor/subcontractor agreements before service.
- Notify the covered entity of unauthorized use, security incidents and breaches
  under applicable rules and the agreed reporting window [counsel]. Specify
  contacts, information, cooperation and who handles each external notification.
- Assist access, amendments, disclosure accounting and any delegated Privacy Rule
  duties within agreed periods [annex]. Make required compliance records available
  to HHS. Identify the designated-record-set scope.
- Bind relevant subcontractors to applicable restrictions and safeguards. Keep
  the approved vendor list and change process in the annex.
- Establish material-breach cure/termination rights. On termination return or
  destroy PHI where feasible; document infeasibility and continuing restrictions.
- [Counsel: indemnities, responsibility allocation, statutory terms, conflicts,
  survival, governing law, signature and state-law formalities.]

Reference: [HHS sample provisions](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/sample-business-associate-agreement-provisions/index.html).

## DPA review template (where controller/processor roles apply)

Controller: [practice/entity]. Processor: [entity]. If Cozy Health independently
determines a purpose, counsel must document the separate controller arrangement
rather than force it into this template.

Annex A: subject matter, duration, nature/purposes, data categories, data subjects,
controller rights/obligations and documented lawful instructions. Annex B:
implemented technical/organizational safeguards. Annex C: subprocessors,
locations, approvals and transfer mechanism. Annex D: retention/rights workflow.

- Process only documented lawful instructions, including transfers; notify the
  controller if an instruction appears unlawful, subject to applicable law.
- Bind authorized personnel to confidentiality and appropriate access controls.
- Implement agreed security measures; require authorization/change notification
  for subprocessors, equivalent obligations and the required responsibility terms.
- Assist rights requests and security, breach, DPIA and consultation duties;
  processor incidents are escalated without undue delay, under agreed procedures.
- At service end return or delete data as instructed unless lawful retention is
  required; address copies, backups, legal holds and verification explicitly.
- Provide compliance evidence and permit agreed audits/inspections.
- [Counsel: controller/processor liability, transfer instruments, dispute terms,
  notifications, local addenda and signatures.]

Reference: [GDPR Article 28 and transfer provisions](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng).
Signatures: [authorized signers, roles, date, agreement and annex versions].
