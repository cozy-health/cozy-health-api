# Provider Terms of Service — draft

Unapproved v0.1, 2026-10-09. Parties: [Cozy Health legal entity and address] and
[provider/practice legal entity]. Effective date: [date]. Governing law: [counsel].

1. **Service.** The portal supports patient-authorized wellbeing information,
   provider notes, assigned resources and reports. It is not an emergency service,
   continuously monitored channel, diagnostic system or substitute for clinical
   judgment. The provider remains responsible for care and applicable professional
   obligations. Do not promise response times the service cannot deliver.
2. **Eligibility.** Provide accurate identity, practice and license information;
   use an individual account. Verification requirements and permitted jurisdictions
   must be agreed before access. A dashboard badge is not a clinical endorsement.
3. **Permitted use.** Access only your verified links and authorized data for the
   agreed care purpose. Respect sharing changes; do not circumvent restrictions,
   scrape patient information, share accounts or use the data for unrelated
   advertising, profiling or training. Apply minimum necessary access.
4. **Security.** Protect devices and downloaded files, restrict staff access, sign
   out on shared devices and report suspected compromise promptly to [security
   contact]. Browser sessions expire after 30 minutes of elapsed time even during
   active use; save notes before expiry. MFA is not currently implemented.
5. **Patient control.** Patients choose each sharing category and may withdraw it
   or disconnect. Subsequent service requests stop returning withdrawn data;
   documents already obtained cannot be recalled. Handle existing copies according
   to the applicable lawful basis, approved retention and professional obligations.
6. **Records and rights.** Follow the executed agreement's retention schedule,
   legal holds and rights-request process. Do not use the portal as the sole legally
   required clinical record. Coordinate access, correction and deletion requests
   with [privacy contact]; requests may require identity and authority checks.
7. **Regulated processing.** Execute any required BAA, DPA and transfer terms before
   regulated access. These terms alone do not establish regulatory compliance or
   replace those agreements.
8. **Suspension and termination.** Cozy Health may suspend unsafe or unauthorized
   access; investigate promptly and communicate through [contact]. On termination,
   cease access and return/delete data as the applicable agreement requires, subject
   to documented lawful retention. Revocation is distinct from record destruction.
9. **Commercial and dispute terms.** [Counsel: fees, service availability, warranties,
   liability, indemnities, notices, dispute venue, modification/termination notice
   periods and any consumer/professional protections.] No blanket waiver is approved.
10. **Acceptance.** [Provider signer, authority, version, timestamp, signature]. The
    current portal does not collect this proof; approved acceptance must be added
    or obtained by an approved documented process before onboarding.
