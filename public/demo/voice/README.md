# Demo voice journals

These two MP3 fixtures narrate the matching fictional journal entries in
DemoAccountSeeder. Generated locally with Windows SpeechSynthesizer (Microsoft
Zira Desktop) and encoded as MP3 with FFmpeg. Neither recording contains real
user data. Duration: approximately eight seconds each.

`small-pause.mp3`: Today I took a short walk after lunch. I noticed the breeze
and gave myself a few quiet minutes before going back to work.

`evening-reflection.mp3`: This evening I made tea and read a few pages of a book.
I am learning that a simple routine can help me settle after a busy day.
